Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Leo's Office is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload the owner's videos, edit their titles and descriptions, manage the owner's playlists and read view statistics for the owner's own channels.

What it stores

Login tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computers. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Leo86868/leos-office
