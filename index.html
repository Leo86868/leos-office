Leo's Office

Leo's Office is a small internal tool used by one creator to publish their own short films to their own YouTube channels (The Living Gallery, The Backstory, The Scam Files) through the official YouTube Data API.

It uploads videos the creator made, sets titles and descriptions, and reads view statistics for those same channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
